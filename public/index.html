Important Regulatory Notice: Emergency Calling (911 / 112 / 999) Limitations

SimlyX is an Over-The-Top (OTT) Voice-over-IP (VoIP) and virtual messaging software service provided by SimlyX Ltd. SimlyX is NOT a replacement for traditional landline or mobile cellular telephone service and DOES NOT support or carry calls to emergency services numbers (including 911 in the United States & Canada, 112 in the European Union, or 999 in the United Kingdom). Users must maintain access to standard mobile or fixed-line telecommunications for emergency service calls.
