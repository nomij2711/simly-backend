Telecommunication Billing Transparency

Virtual phone numbers and VoIP minutes are real-time telecommunication services provisioned immediately across carrier switches upon payment. Due to wholesale carrier commitments, line allocations are non-refundable once activated.

SECTION 1

1. Digital Consumables Overview

When a user purchases a virtual phone line (e.g., USA, UK, Canada, Australia) or purchases prepaid VoIP minutes, SimlyX incurs immediate non-recoverable carrier routing and switch reservation costs. Consequently, telecommunications services are classified as digital consumables delivered in real time upon purchase.

SECTION 2

2. Virtual Number Subscriptions

  • Non-Refundable Line Rental: Monthly virtual number rental fees ($1.50/mo for USA, $2.00/mo for UK & Canada, $4.50/mo for Australia) are strictly non-refundable once the number is assigned to your account.
  • Third-Party OTP Compatibility: Because third-party platforms (banks, social media) maintain autonomous verification algorithms, failure of a specific external service to accept a VoIP number does not qualify for a cash refund.
SECTION 3

3. Wallet Balances & Unused Credits

If you topped up your SimlyX balance and have not made any phone calls or sent SMS messages from that top-up balance, you are eligible to request a full refund to your original payment method within 14 calendar days of the transaction date.

SECTION 4

4. Cancellation & Auto-Renewal Management

You have complete autonomy over your subscriptions. There are zero long-term lock-in contracts:

  • You can disable monthly line auto-renewal anytime in the SimlyX mobile app settings.
  • Upon disabling renewal, your number remains fully active until the end of the current paid billing cycle.
  • Once the billing cycle concludes, the number is decommissioned and returned to the global carrier pool.
SECTION 5

5. System Provisioning Failures

In the rare event that a payment is completed but our carrier provisioning switch fails to allocate an active number due to a technical server error, our billing team will promptly issue a replacement number or a 100% full refund immediately upon verification.

SECTION 6

6. Billing Support & Refund Inquiries
