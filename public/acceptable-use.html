Zero-Tolerance Anti-Abuse Policy

SimlyX is dedicated to maintaining high carrier reputation and network integrity. Any use of our virtual lines for spam, robocalling, fraud, or harassment is strictly prohibited and results in immediate account termination without refund.

1. Permitted Uses

SimlyX virtual phone lines are intended strictly for lawful personal and commercial telecommunications, including:

  • Personal privacy and separating business communications on a single device.
  • Receiving legitimate One-Time Passwords (OTPs) and 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) codes.
  • Making and receiving standard point-to-point voice calls and individual SMS text messages.
  • Remote freelancing, e-commerce, and business communication.

2. Strictly Prohibited Telecom Activities

Users must not engage in any of the following activities on the SimlyX network:

  • Unsolicited Bulk SMS / Spam: Dispatching unsolicited mass text messages in violation of CTIA, TCPA, or 10DLC regulations.
  • Automated Robocalling & Predictive Dialers: Using automated dialers, voice broadcasting systems, or robocall scripts.
  • Deceptive Caller ID Spoofing: Transmitting inaccurate or misleading caller ID information to deceive or defraud call recipients.
  • Phishing & Financial Fraud: Impersonating financial institutions, government agencies, or tech support to solicit sensitive credentials or funds.
  • Harassment & Abusive Communications: Transmitting threatening, defamatory, or abusive voice calls or text messages.

3. Carrier Monitoring & Automatic Blacklisting

Our Tier-1 carrier partners (including Telnyx LLC) deploy real-time automated fraud detection systems. Lines exhibiting high spam complaint rates, abnormal high-frequency dialing, or short-duration call bursts are automatically flagged, blacklisted, and reclaimed immediately.

4. Reporting Violations

To report spam, abuse, or harassment originating from a SimlyX number, please email our Trust & Safety team with relevant timestamps and message details at support@simlyx.com.
