Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 • We respect your privacy and never sell your data.

Our Privacy Commitment: Zero Data Selling

SimlyX is built on privacy-first infrastructure. We never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your private phone calls, SMS content, or contact details to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

1. Information We Collect

To provide telecommunications routing and maintain active phone lines, SimlyX collects:

  • Account Details: Email address, username, encrypted password hash, and customer identifier (e.g. SIM-XXXXXX).
  • Telecommunication Metadata: Call detail records (call start time, duration, destination prefix) strictly required for carrier billing and PSTN interconnect transit.
  • Payment Information: Transaction timestamps, gateway reference IDs, and payment method type. We do not store raw credit card numbers; all billing is processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways (Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Binance Pay).
  • Technical Data: Device model, operating system version, and IP address used for fraud prevention and security authentication.

2. End-to-End Signaling & Media Encryption

All SIP signaling and WebRTC audio transmissions are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) and Secure Real-Time Transport Protocol (SRTP). Database records at rest are secured with industry-standard AES-256 encryption.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To allocate and manage virtual telephone numbers across global Tier-1 carrier networks.
  • To transmit and deliver incoming SMS 2FA verification codes to your inbox in real time.
  • To prevent fraudulent robocalling, account takeover, or illegal telecommunication abuse.
  • To process billing top-ups and subscription auto-renewals.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Right to Erasure)

Under GDPR (Articles 17 & 20) and CCPA regulations, you have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal records. You may initiate account deletion directly within the mobile application settings or by emailing privacy@simlyx.com. Upon confirmation, all personal identifiers are permanently purged within 30 days.

5. Third-Party Carrier Disclosures

SimlyX works with licensed Tier-1 telecommunications carriers (such as Telnyx LLC) to interconnect calls and SMS with global public switched telephone networks (PSTN). Transit metadata is shared exclusively as necessary to establish voice connections and route SMS messages according to CTIA standards.

6. Privacy Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data protection rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@simlyx.com.
