Delete Account & Data

Request permanent deletion of your SimlyX profile, virtual telephone numbers, Call Detail Records, and personal records.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

Upon verification of your deletion request, the following actions will be executed across our infrastructure and carrier switches:

  • Virtual Numbers Deactivation: All active virtual numbers assigned to your account will be detached, quarantined, and released back to the global carrier pool.
  • Message & Call Logs Purge: Inbound/outbound SMS verification messages, call logs, and voicemail records are permanently deleted from database storage.
  • Account Profile & Authentication: Your email address, customer identifier (SIM-XXXXXX), and security tokens are permanently erased.
  • Wallet Balances: Any remaining prepaid calling credits will be forfeited upon account closure.

Method 1: Inside Mobile App

The fastest way to delete your account is directly inside the SimlyX App:

  1. Open the SimlyX App.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Store Compliance & Legal.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Erase Data.
  5. Confirm deletion.

Method 2: Web Request Form

If you uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, submit the deletion request form below or email support@simlyx.com.

Verification processing timeframe: Within 30 calendar days (GDPR standard).
