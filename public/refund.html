Digital Consumables Policy Overview

Virtual phone numbers and VoIP calling minutes are digital telecommunication services provisioned immediately through licensed carrier networks. Because wholesale allocation costs are incurred instantly upon purchase, virtual number subscription fees are non-refundable once line activation is completed.

1. Virtual Number Subscriptions

When you purchase a virtual phone number (e.g. USA, UK, Canada, Australia), our system immediately reserves and registers that line exclusively to your account on the global carrier PSTN switch.

  • Non-Refundable: Monthly line rental fees are non-refundable once the line has been activated.
  • Third-Party OTP Filtering: Consistent with telecom standards, no refunds will be issued if a specific third-party platform (e.g. particular bank or app) enforces proprietary VoIP restrictions on a number.
  • Technical Provisioning Failure: In verified cases where a line failed to activate due to an internal system error, a replacement number or full credit refund will be provided immediately.

2. Unused Wallet Balances

If you have topped up your account wallet balance and have not used any credits for outgoing calls or SMS messages, you may request a refund to your original payment method within 14 days of purchase by contacting support.

3. Subscription Cancellation & Auto-Renewal

There are zero long-term commitments. You may cancel your monthly number renewal at any time directly through the SimlyX mobile app. Upon cancellation, your line remains active until the end of your current paid billing period, after which it will not renew and will be released back to the global carrier pool.

4. Requesting a Billing Review

For any billing questions or refund requests, please email our support team with your account email and transaction ID at support@simlyx.com. All reviews are handled within 24 to 48 business hours.
