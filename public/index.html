Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SimlyX virtual numbers and service.

What is SimlyX and how does it work?

SimlyX is a virtual telephone number service provided by SimlyX Ltd. It provides real private telephone numbers in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and 50+ countries that work over your internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data). You do not need a physical SIM card or extra hardware.

Can I use SimlyX numbers for SMS 2FA verifications?

Yes! SimlyX numbers are connected directly to Tier-1 carrier PSTN networks to reliably receive SMS verification codes and 2FA OTPs for services like WhatsApp, Telegram, Google, OpenAI, Apple, and social platforms.

How many numbers can I have on one device?

There are no restrictions. You can purchase and maintain unlimited virtual phone numbers across multiple countries on a single smartphone.

Are incoming calls and text messages free?

Yes! All incoming voice calls and incoming SMS messages to your SimlyX virtual numbers are 100% free with zero roaming charges worldwide.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, there are zero long-term contracts. You can renew your number monthly or cancel at any time directly through the app dashboard.
